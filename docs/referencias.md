# Referências

## Mesmo dispositivo / mesma plataforma
- [entitymar/annblackbox-custom-firmware-installer](https://github.com/entitymar/annblackbox-custom-firmware-installer) – instalador de `.fwsc` customizado + V20 de fábrica
- [entitymar/MVAVE-ANNBLACKBOX-FIRMWARE-UPDATE-SOFTWARE-decompiled](https://github.com/entitymar/MVAVE-ANNBLACKBOX-FIRMWARE-UPDATE-SOFTWARE-decompiled) – updater descompilado
- [lucidsoup/BlackboxFX](https://github.com/lucidsoup/BlackboxFX) – firmware custom para o BlackBox (repositório ainda vazio em 2026-10-22; acompanhar)
- [AL-255/FM-1-RE](https://github.com/AL-255/FM-1-RE) – RE completo do M-VAVE FM-1 (AC791N, pi32v2, OTA, Ghidra)

## Firmwares abertos na mesma plataforma (instalados só por USB)
- [hugelton/Felucca](https://github.com/hugelton/Felucca) – firmware GPL-3.0 do FM-1; toolchain no macOS via Docker, loader, instalador web, 2º núcleo — **base do nosso port**
- [charlesvestal/fm1-x0x](https://github.com/charlesvestal/fm1-x0x) – fork do Felucca com modo de segurança
- [genkigenki/fm1-to-smk37](https://github.com/genkigenki/fm1-to-smk37) – kit de port FM-1 → SMK-37 (outra placa AC791N)
- [m-vave/fm1_open](https://github.com/m-vave/fm1_open) – código aberto oficial da M-VAVE para o FM-1
- [aroum/fm1-custom-fw](https://github.com/aroum/fm1-custom-fw) – índice de firmwares e ferramentas
